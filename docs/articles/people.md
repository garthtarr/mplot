# mplot contributors

> Details of those who have contributed to the mplot project.

Core development team:

- Dr Garth Tarr (University of Sydney)
- Prof Samuel Mueller (University of Sydney)
- Prof Alan Welsh (Australian National University)

Suggestions and testing:

- Dr Kevin Murray (University of Western Australia)

This research was undertaken with the assistance of resources from the
National Computational Infrastructure (NCI), which is supported by the
Australian Government. Samuel Mueller and Alan Welsh were supported by
the Australian Research Council (DP140101259). We also gratefully
acknowledge two anonymous referees for their helpful comments and
suggestions for the paper and package.

#### Citation

If you use this package to inform your model selection choices, please
use the following citation:

``` r

citation("mplot")
```

    To cite mplot in publications use:

      Tarr G, Müller S, Welsh AH (2018). "mplot: An R Package for Graphical
      Model Stability and Variable Selection Procedures." _Journal of
      Statistical Software_, *83*(9), 1-28. doi:10.18637/jss.v083.i09
      <https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v083.i09>.
